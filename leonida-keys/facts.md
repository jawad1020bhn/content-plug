# Facts — verified, with official/source links (never post a fact that isn't here)

- GTA 6 launches Nov 19, 2026; New Zealand and Australia unlock first — source: plugin README launch plan / Rockstar marketing. Status: verified (launch day).
- GTA 6 is set in the state of **Leonida** (Rockstar's fictional Florida) with six officially named regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga National Park — source: https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-interactive-map (2026-11-19)
- **The story begins in the Leonida Keys** — Jason Duval lives and works there at the southern tip of the map; a chain of islands based on the real Florida Keys, connected by long overwater causeway bridges — sources: https://gta6locations.com/leonida-keys/ (2026-11-15), https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-interactive-map
- **Key Lento** is the officially named stilt-house community in the Leonida Keys where Jason lives (shown in trailer 2 and official screenshots, on land owned by Brian Heder); name plays on Key Largo — source: https://gta6locations.com/leonida-keys/ (2026-11-15)
- Supporting cast embedded in the Keys island economy: Brian Heder, Cal Hampton — source: https://gta6locations.com/leonida-keys/
- Boats and small planes are the main way around the Keys (car travel loses appeal) — source: https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-interactive-map
