(The Largest Community Iftar in the World – Matariya Iftar) Around 40 streets in the Matariya district were transformed into one giant Ramadan iftar table, welcoming more than 100,000 fasting people during the annual community iftar organized by the residents of the area. The iftar tables stretched across long distances through the streets, while volunteers from the neighborhood’s youth and residents worked since early hours preparing and arranging tables and chairs to welcome visitors coming from different parts of Cairo to participate in this unique event. During the preparations, the people of Matariya successfully prepared nearly 100,000 iftar meals, which were distributed to fasting attendees at the moment of Maghrib prayer, in a Ramadan atmosphere filled with joy, generosity, and the spirit of sharing. The event was attended by a large number of visitors and guests who were eager to witness this remarkable occasion up close, as the area turned into a vibrant festival reflecting the generosity of Egyptians and their strong sense of community during the holy month of Ramadan.
